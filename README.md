A one-page front page summing up world news on Monday 23 November 2026: the AI rout
that hit Asia (Kospi −3.3%), Guterres' call to regulate AI before it's too late, and the
US lead over China falling to a record low in the Asia Power Index — plus 10 more stories,
18 also-on-Monday items, a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box separating
23 November news from stories dated other days.

- `entry.png` — the finished 1024×1024 front page (31 stories, 6 regions)
- `draw.py` — the PIL script that draws it
- `main.py` — stdlib-only runner: validates the PNG and copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
